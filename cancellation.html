Appointment information

Cancellation & rescheduling.

Plans change. Current cancellation, rescheduling, deposit and no-show terms are provided through Carli’s secure GlossGenius booking page and appointment communications.

Current terms

The terms displayed during booking, in your confirmation, or in later appointment messages are the terms that apply to your appointment. Please review them before completing your reservation.

Changing an appointment

Use the manage-appointment link in your GlossGenius confirmation whenever available. If you need assistance, contact Carli through the contact method included in your booking confirmation.

View booking page ↗

Late arrivals and missed appointments

Late arrivals may require a shortened or rescheduled service depending on the remaining appointment time. Any applicable charges or deposit treatment are governed by the terms associated with the booking.
