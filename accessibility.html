An inclusive experience

Accessibility statement.

Carli Special is committed to making its website and salon information welcoming and usable for as many people as possible.

Our approach

The website is designed with semantic page structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, descriptive image text, readable typography and responsive layouts. Accessibility is reviewed as the site changes.

External booking

Online booking is provided by GlossGenius and is governed by that service’s accessibility features and policies. If the booking experience creates a barrier, please contact Carli through the official Instagram account or the contact method included in previous appointment communications.

Need assistance?

If you have difficulty accessing information, selecting a service or arranging an appointment, please let Carli know what page or task caused the problem and what type of assistance would be helpful.

Contact on Instagram ↗

Ongoing improvement

Automated testing and manual keyboard review are used as practical checks, but accessibility is an ongoing process. Feedback is welcome and will be used to improve the experience.
