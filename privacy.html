Privacy policy.

This website is designed to provide information about Carli Special and direct clients to trusted services for booking and social media.

Information collected on this site

Carli Special does not currently operate contact forms, user accounts, online payments or appointment databases on this website. The site does not directly request or store personal information from visitors.

External services

Booking links open GlossGenius, and social links may open Instagram. Those companies may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Information submitted on those services is handled by the applicable service provider.

Technical information

The website host and ordinary internet infrastructure may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type and request logs, for security and reliable delivery. Some images or resources may also be delivered by third-party content hosts.

Future changes

If analytics, forms, direct booking, cookies or other data-collection features are added later, this policy should be updated before those features are launched.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact Carli through the contact information supplied with your appointment or through the official Carli Special Instagram account.
